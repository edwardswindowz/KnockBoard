    Payments & invoices

    Add any way you want to get paid. Customers see only the ones you fill in. Square and Stripe need a payment link from your account (use one where the buyer sets the price). Cash App, Venmo and Zelle just need your name or number.
    Where do I find my Square payment link?
    1. Open the Square Point of Sale app on your phone.
    2. Tap More, then Payment links (or Create link).
    3. Choose Collect a payment.
    4. Turn on Allow buyer to set the price. That lets each customer pay their own invoice amount.
    5. Tap Create link, then Copy link.
    6. Come back here and paste it in the box above.
    Menu names can look a little different depending on your Square version. You only need to do this once.
    Where do I find my Stripe payment link?
    1. Log in at dashboard.stripe.com on your phone or computer.
    2. Go to Payment links and tap New.
    3. Add a product and choose Customers choose what to pay, so each customer can enter their invoice amount.
    4. Tap Create link, then Copy link.
    5. Come back here and paste it in the box above.
    Only fill this in if you use Stripe. You only need to do this once.
